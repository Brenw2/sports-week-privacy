# Sports Week Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sports Week app ("Sports Week," "the app," "we," "us") handles information. Sports Week is developed by Brennan Wade.

## Using the app without an account

Accounts are optional. You can use Sports Week as a guest without creating an account.

## Information we store if you sign in

If you choose to create an account or sign in, using email or Sign in with Apple, we store:

- **Account identity:** your email address and/or the identifier provided by Sign in with Apple, managed through Supabase Auth.
- **Followed teams:** the teams you choose to follow.
- **Preferences:** your app settings, such as appearance.

We use this information to provide your account and to sync your followed teams and preferences across your devices.

## Sports data

Schedules, scores, and standings shown in the app come from third-party sports data providers, such as ESPN and similar public feeds. This sports content is not your personal data.

## Where your data is stored

Account data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase (authentication and Postgres database), hosted in the United States (AWS us-east-1).

## How we share data

We do not sell your personal data. Sports Week does not currently show ads.

## Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account in the app using **Delete account**. This removes your account and the associated profile, followed teams, and preferences.

You can also ask us to delete your account and data by emailing [brenw2@gmail.com](mailto:brenw2@gmail.com).

## Children

Sports Week is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version at this page and update the effective date above.
